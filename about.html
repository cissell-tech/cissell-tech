Grounded Experience, Local Commitment

Born and raised right here in Marion, AR, I’ve spent over two decades navigating the evolving landscapes of IT management, healthcare technology, and Managed Service Provider (MSP) operations.

Throughout my career managing technical infrastructure, direct engineering teams, and complex client implementations, one challenge consistently stood out: small businesses are frequently underserved. They are often handed generic, one-size-fits-all software packages or shuffled between tiered call centers where no one truly understands their operational context.

I founded Cissell Technologies to change that dynamic. We act as a dedicated technology partner—providing the senior expertise, practical security, and ongoing care of an internal IT department, grounded in personal accountability and transparent local relationship building.
