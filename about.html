David Cissell

Grounded Local Presence, Enterprise Capability

Living right across the bridge in Marion, AR, I’ve spent over two decades navigating IT infrastructure, healthcare technology systems, and Managed Service Provider (MSP) operations.

Small markets across our region have historically kept large corporate MSPs at arm’s length—and for good reason. National providers often try to push aggressive subscription models, impose one-size-fits-all cloud shifts, or treat local teams like a number in a distant queue. As a result, many local businesses hesitate to adopt emerging technology like modern cloud architectures or AI automation, worried about complexity and loss of control.

I built Cissell Technologies to serve as that gateway. With one foot firmly planted in the world of modern enterprise IT and the other right here in our local community, I help Mid-South businesses adopt modern, secure technology at a sensible, comfortable pace—backed by direct accountability and personal partnership.
